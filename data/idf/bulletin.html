Journeys = ordered pairs of stations joined by a step-free route (boardable platforms only, every boarding/alighting point reachable from the street by working lifts). Repair gain = journeys confirmed again if that one lift worked, all else equal; equal gains are ordered by the station's daily usage (Validations sur le réseau ferré : Nombre de validations par jour (2ème trimestre 2026)). Lift status: SNCF hourly feed for SNCF lifts, IDFM « État des ascenseurs » for RATP lifts.
